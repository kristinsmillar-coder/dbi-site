Admissions

A selective program. A serious commitment. Applications open only at the start of the school year in the fall.

The Flagship Program · DBI’s main program

A place in the cohort
is earned through selection.

Cathedral Catholic students apply for a limited fall cohort of 50. Admission involves a rigorous application review and interview process.

Fall applications only

New students may apply when school starts in the fall. DBI does not accept applications year-round or offer rolling enrollment. Follow Cathedral Catholic’s start-of-year announcements for the application window and deadline.

  1. 01

    Written application

    During the fall window, submit an application demonstrating your interest, commitment, and readiness to contribute. Applications are reviewed before interview invitations are issued.

  2. 02

    Interview by invitation

    Selected applicants advance to an interview. The process considers curiosity, follow-through, communication, coachability, and teamwork.

  3. 03

    Final cohort selection

    Places are limited. Applying or receiving an interview does not guarantee admission. Selected students commit to the sessions, team preparation, and Case Competition.

Understand the Flagship commitment

Before you apply

Understand the process.

When can a new student apply?

Only in the fall, when the school year starts. The program is not open for year-round applications. The school announces the specific window and deadline.

Who is eligible?

Cathedral Catholic students in grades 9–12 may apply during the fall window. Eligibility is the starting point; admission still requires the full selection process.

Is previous business experience required?

No. Selection focuses on curiosity, follow-through, communication, coachability, and teamwork. Applicants should demonstrate a serious commitment to learning and contributing.

Does fall admission include the spring term?

Not automatically. Current Flagship students seeking to enter spring entrepreneurship go through a separate venture pitch and interview process. This is an internal progression, not a second application window for new students.

How is Finance different?

Flagship is DBI’s main business and entrepreneurship program. Finance is a smaller, 25-student offshoot focused on financial literacy and investing. It does not include the Flagship Case Competition, entrepreneurship term, or Startup Summit. Its new-student application window is also at the start of school in the fall.

What commitment does Flagship require?

Admitted students attend scheduled sessions, prepare with their teams, and participate in the Case Competition. Students selected for spring then develop a venture with their mentors and prepare for the Startup Summit.

Additional program
