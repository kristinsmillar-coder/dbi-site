One demanding journey.

Fall · The Flagship capstone

The Case Competition

A real company brings the problem. Your team brings the recommendation.

Students research the business, compare strategic options, work through the numbers, and present their case on stage to company leadership and judges.

In 2025, Surfline challenged teams to choose an expansion market: Indonesia, South Africa, or Brazil. Students had to defend both where the company should go and how it could succeed.

Inside the Case Competition

Spring · The entrepreneurship finale

The Dons
Startup Summit

This time, it’s your company.

After a separate pitch and interview process, selected Flagship students develop a business with guidance from industry entrepreneurs and USD MBA mentors.

At the Summit, they pitch their ventures to an experienced judging panel in front of families and the school community, competing for awards that help them keep building.

2026 Summit 10 student companies · 19 founders · $19,000 in awards

Selective admission · Fall entry only

A rigorous application process.
A committed cohort.

Flagship admission requires a reviewed written application, an invitation to interview for selected applicants, and a final selection for a limited cohort. Interest alone does not guarantee a place.

Applications open only at the start of the school year in the fall. There is no year-round enrollment. Continuing into spring entrepreneurship requires a separate selection process for current Flagship students.

A focused, 25-student program in financial literacy and investing. It has its own projects and does not include Flagship’s Case Competition, entrepreneurship term, or Startup Summit.
