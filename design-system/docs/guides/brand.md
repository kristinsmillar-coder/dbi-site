# The DBI brand

The Dons Business Institute is Cathedral Catholic High School's student-led
business institute (San Diego). The design language comes from the live site,
cathedralcatholicdbi.org, and it is deliberate about very few things, strictly:

## Color means something

- **Dons red `#BA0C2F`** (Pantone 200C, from the school's own palette) — the
  school. It marks **exactly one thing per screen**: the primary action, the
  active state, the rule above a quote. Never atmosphere, never decoration.
- **Gold `#FFCB30`** — the award. Prize money, the winning step, celebration
  eyebrows, the stats that prove the program. On light grounds its job passes
  to **gold-ink `#8A6A00`** (pure gold vanishes on paper).
- **Two grounds**: near-black `#0B0A0B` (the theater) and warm paper
  `#E8E4E1` / white (the classroom). Pages alternate between them. Neutrals
  carry a red bias so they read as chosen.

## Type

- **Young Serif** — display. Headings are sentence-case declarations that end
  with a period ("Applications are open.").
- **Work Sans** — body.
- **DM Mono, uppercase, letterspaced** — the metadata voice: eyebrows,
  datelines, labels, buttons, captions.

## Shape

Square corners everywhere. No border radius, no drop shadows, no gradients as
decoration. Structure comes from 1px hairline rules (`#241f20` on dark,
`#E7E0DA` on light) and ledger-like alignment.

## Voice

Short declarative sentences. Concrete numbers over adjectives ("~200
applications reviewed", never "many applicants"). The Flagship Program is the
headline act; the Finance Program is presented as the smaller, deliberate
offshoot.
