## Photography

Real photography only — DBI never uses AI-generated or stock imagery.
The library is real students, real sessions, real judges, shot at Cathedral
Catholic. Much of the high-end photography is black and white; color shots
keep the red/gold palette company. Reference set shipped with this system
(`assets/photos/`):

## Treatment

- Photos sit borderless on white surfaces, or under dark scrims on the black
  ground when copy sits over them.
- B&W editorial pairs (e.g. presenting + judges as a diptych with mono
  captions) are a house move.
- Captions are small DM Mono, dim, sentence case.
