Mentorship

Someone in your corner.

Each spring venture team works with a USD MBA mentor alongside an industry entrepreneur. Students have people to turn to as they test ideas, make decisions, and prepare to pitch.

College coursework

Ahead of the next chapter.

DBI nominates students for opportunities to take business coursework through USD’s Knauss School of Business while they’re still at Cathedral Catholic.

Leadership

A shared investment.

Knauss School Dean Brian Tietje, PhD, serves on DBI’s advisory board and has joined our students as a fall keynote speaker.

Rooted in our history

Once Uni.
Always connected.

Our connection goes back to the beginning.

Cathedral Catholic opened in 1957 as University of San Diego High School—“Uni.” Today, the DBI partnership continues that relationship through mentorship, shared learning, and new opportunities for Dons.
