An update on your application

You’re in.

Welcome to the 2026 Flagship cohort of the Dons Business Institute.

Scroll for your letter ↓

Congratulations!

We are excited to inform you that you have been tentatively accepted into the 2026 Dons Business Institute Flagship Program cohort.

This year’s Flagship Program will consist of just 50 students. Our team spent more than 900 minutes, or 15 hours, reviewing applications and conducting the admissions process, and selecting this year’s cohort was an extremely challenging decision. We were incredibly impressed by the quality of applicants, and we are excited to welcome you into this year’s program.

Your acceptance is considered tentative while Cathedral Catholic’s Dean of Discipline completes a final review of each accepted student’s record. This review is conducted to confirm that students have no academic dishonesty violations or other disciplinary concerns that would affect their eligibility to participate in DBI.

Once this final review is complete, your acceptance will be officially confirmed.

Please block out these dates now

As a member of the Flagship cohort, you should immediately reserve the following dates on your calendar:

  • Session 1 — September 222:45–6:30 PM
  • Session 2 — October 132:45–6:30 PM
  • Session 3 — October 202:45–6:30 PM
  • Session 4, Case Practice — November 192:45–6:30 PM
  • Case Competition Rehearsal — December 12:45–6:30 PM
  • Case Competition — December 22:45–8:00 PM

These dates are an important part of the DBI Flagship Program, so please make sure they are blocked off now and avoid scheduling other commitments during these times.

Add all six dates to my calendar

We will be reaching out soon with additional information, including syllabus and required forms, the DBI orientation date, and next steps for the start of the program.

Congratulations again on earning a place in this year’s DBI Flagship cohort. We look forward to an incredible year ahead.
