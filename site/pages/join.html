A few things to know

Bring questions.
We have a few answers.

What do you look for in an applicant?

Curiosity, follow-through, communication, coachability, and teamwork. We’re interested in how you approach learning—not how much business vocabulary you already know.

Can I apply to both programs?

Yes. Use each program’s application form. The Flagship and Finance programs have different learning paths and selection processes.

What is the time commitment?

Plan to attend the scheduled sessions and participate fully in team projects. The program pages include fall dates, session times, and calendar downloads.

How does the application process work?

Start with the program’s application. Selected applicants are invited to an interview. Flagship students apply and interview again for the spring entrepreneurship term.

Is the Finance Program part of Flagship?

Finance is a separate, yearlong program. It does not include the Flagship Case Competition, spring entrepreneurship term, or Startup Summit prize pool.
