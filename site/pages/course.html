The Startup Summit

A big moment.
A bigger beginning.

Students bring their companies to the stage to pitch before a panel of judges. It’s a celebration of the work—and a chance to see just how far an idea can go.

At the 2026 Summit, 10 student companies competed for $19,000 in awards. Uji Energy, DietLabs, and PurposeForge earned the top three places, with R&R Threads receiving the People’s Choice Award.
